# Privacy Policy — Tiny Road Trip

_Last updated: `<date>`_

`<Developer name>` ("we") made Tiny Road Trip ("the game"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

## Information we collect

The game does not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There is no account, sign-up, login, chat, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only:
- coins
- best distance
- sound on/off setting
- whether the tutorial hint has been shown

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may collect limited, anonymous technical data, such as device model, operating system version and engine performance statistics, to improve its engine. See Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

The game shows no ads, offers no in-app purchases and includes no other third-party analytics or tracking.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
